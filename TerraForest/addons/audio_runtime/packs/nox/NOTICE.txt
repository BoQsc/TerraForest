This directory contains 80 selected individual footsteps converted to 48 kHz
mono PCM16 and two complete ambience/fire loops converted to 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis.
The .bin suffix preserves the codec bytes through Godot export; these are WAV
and Ogg files, not executable resources. No promotional artwork was imported.
The source archive is retained in the external development source-asset cache.

TerraForest's original pack.audio.module.json authoring data and example pack metadata are
dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. NOX recordings retain the author's
CC0 dedication. Attribution is retained voluntarily for provenance.
The example_audio/soft_grass.wav.bin copy derives from grass_walk_01.wav.bin;
its original archive member and hashes are recorded in provenance.json here.
