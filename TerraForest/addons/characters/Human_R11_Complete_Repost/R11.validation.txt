R11 — OPTIONAL RIGHT-HAND-LED PICKAXE GRIP
========================================
Recorded UTC: 2026-10-19T01:15:38.317715+00:00

DELIVERED BEHAVIOR
------------------
Select Swing styles > Pickaxe grip > Right hand + left support (experimental).
Choose Return to live controls, equip with 2, and swing with the left mouse button.
The right hand takes the upper/leading station; the left hand supports the
lower handle from the character's left side. Original remains the startup
grip. Grip choice is independent of the default, right-lean and tool-tilt
swing-style choices. All three pickaxe styles have matching new grip clips.

The optional grip includes idle, walk, run, jump, swing, first-person
draw/stow and held third-person crouch. Live changes wait through active
actions, jumping and crouch exit, then lower/redraw the tool. Paused
inspection maps the matching clip at the current time for direct comparison.
During live switching, the hands lower mostly below the first-person frame
while the head stays visible. The different setbacks produce a progressive
apparent-size change before the hands rise into the newly selected grip.

The first-person tool moves 6 cm toward the body for reach. At the fully
lowered draw/stow endpoint this becomes 15 cm, with the support station 3 cm
higher on the handle, fading back to the ordinary station by Ready.
This changes the camera crop; the lower support hand and portions of the
tool head are outside the view in some poses. The right/left side views
are needed to assess parts hidden from the first-person camera.

Tool rotation and swing timing are inherited. The new ordinary third-person
clips retain their source tool path. Held crouch combines the existing
crouch body motion with the grip relative to the chest. See the authored
profile and per-clip reports for the actual arm route and measured limits.

PRESERVATION AND CORRECTION SCOPE
---------------------------------
Every existing component of the 54 old animation clips is preserved.
The old shirt-weight outputs are widened with exact-zero new components.
Their complete animation JSON records are therefore not byte-identical:
only the necessary shirt output-accessor references change.

Every newly authored rotation track has consistent adjacent quaternion
signs. Ten antipodal pairs on the hidden Weapon_Axe tracks are inherited
exactly from the source Walk/Run/Jump clips; the audit separately requires
their source sampler, timestamps and decoded values to remain exact.
Their reported rotation steps use shortest-arc orientation distance.

The entire original binary prefix, original accessors/buffer views, base
geometry, rest normals, skin, materials, textures, rest rig and all original
morph target data remain exact. New POSITION fields are confined to the
existing sleeve/underarm correction support. New NORMAL fields may also
affect its incident face neighborhood and coincident exported copies.
There are no new morph TANGENT fields, matching the source convention.
Original base tangents remain exact. This export convention by itself
does not prove exact geometric normals for arbitrary mixtures of shapes.

The native comparison independently reopens the exact R10 baseline first.
All existing curves in 378 original actions remain exact. The 54 old shirt
companions gain constant-zero new curves. Old shape coordinates are
compared by their original names; new coordinates are independently
checked against the authored cache and original sleeve vertex support.

---------------------------

input times in the two new Idle clips. The separate post-bake normalizer
keeps the exact source-breakpoint row at each alias and removes the
redundant paired row. The other 69 exported animation records and the
original static records/binary prefix remain exact to the baked model.
All 17 native-cache schedules are rounded to the emitted Float32 seconds;
only the two Idle schedules lose rows. The native file was then rebuilt.

PROTOTYPE HISTORY
-----------------
Earlier sparse prototypes were rejected after visible sleeve/skin defects
were found in side views. The changed support-elbow route and dedicated
sleeve basis were introduced to address those contacts. Their retained
reports identify the exact images opened and the defects found. Prototype
capture totals are not included in final capture/review totals. Some
completed sparse capture sets are included for manifest integrity; their
inclusion does not imply every prototype image received a visual review.
