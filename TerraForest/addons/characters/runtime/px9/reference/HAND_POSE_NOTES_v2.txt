Grip and arm solution
---------------------
Canonical weapon space is +Y forward and +Z up, in metres. Human Blender
space is -Y forward, +Z up, with anatomical right toward -X.

The right palm rotates +30 degrees about item X. Left support rotates
+25 degrees about item X, then -30 degrees about item Z. The magazine
grip rotates -25 degrees about item X. Each translation preserves its
original Middle1 MCP anchor. Exact matrices are in wrist_refinement_final.json.

Elbow guidance is 85% toward wrist minus palm-Y times the original forearm
length, blended with 15% anatomical pole (side_sign*.38, -.08, 1.22).
Right side_sign=-1; left=+1. Existing fixed bone lengths remain unchanged.
