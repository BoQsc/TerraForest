HAND CONTACT
Right hand retains the exact item-local grip throughout every clip.
Support hand holds the same support station during idle, locomotion, aiming and firing.
Inspection releases the support hand, shows both sides, then regrips.
Reload: support hand reaches magazine by frame 24; carries it through extraction and
off-screen exchange; seats it at frame 112; releases after frame 121 and regrips.
Rack: support hand reaches slide by frame 22; follows slide through frame 41; returns by frame 60.
Every integer-frame contact station is recorded in validation/AUTHORED_FRAMES.json.

VISUAL AND GAMEPLAY SCOPE
One magazine mesh represents outgoing/incoming magazines at the off-screen exchange event.
No duplicate magazine geometry is added. Host ammunition/inventory decisions remain external.
An animated casing is included. Hide EjectedCase if the game spawns its own casing.
Audio, muzzle flash, physical projectiles and damage rules are not included.
The 11 original weapon-only clips, including assemble/disassemble, remain in the weapon GLB.
Their earlier table is reference/Weapon_Original_ANIMATIONS.json.
Assembly/disassembly are visual game presentation, outside the paired first-person set.
