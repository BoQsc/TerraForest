Creator: OpenAI Codex / TerraForest contributors, authored directly at the
maintainer's request for a new automatic assault rifle. Fictional Ranger design:
olive receiver, long fore-end and barrel, open stock, iron sights, curved magazine.
The meshes contain original beveled primitives and flat PBR material parameters.
No outside model, texture, font, audio, animation pack or implementation was copied.
Editable source: tools/author_ranger_rifle.py (0BSD), Ranger.blend.
Authoring tool: existing external Blender 5.2.3 LTS; hashes in Ranger.json.

The new meshes fit the grip and magazine anchors of the existing Human.r11 rig.
Runtime carry, equip, holster, aim and reload clips are reused; three firing clips
are retimed in human_view.gd to 80 ms for automatic cadence. These existing clips,
rig, hands and character textures retain their existing provenance and terms in
addons/characters/LICENSE.txt and docs/LICENSE_INVENTORY.md. They are not contained
in the new standalone Ranger GLBs or Blender file, and are not relicensed here.

Original synthesized shot and procedural muzzle-flash parameters are covered by
addons/player_runtime/COMBAT_PROVENANCE.txt. New runtime/tool/test code is 0BSD.
