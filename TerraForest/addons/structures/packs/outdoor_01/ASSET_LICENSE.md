original geometry, materials, procedural fabric and rendered icon pixels.

the contributors; it does not purport to license third-party rights.

Created with Codex and the existing external Godot 4.7.2 Steam installation on
2026-11-26. Brief: a separate outdoor pack with a bird nest, duffel bag, cardboard
boxes, tires, litter, trash bags, trash cans and a large refuse container, using
native shared rendering and reusable storage. No external models, photographs,
textures, brands or reference images were imported.

The offline author extends the project's Homestead 10 author, which reuses the
Homestead 02/04 primitive helpers and Homestead 07 cushion formula. Their original
0BSD source notices remain intact. No engine source is copied into this pack.
Exact source and output hashes are retained in `provenance.json`; generate or
verify them with `tools/object_content_provenance.py [--check]`.
