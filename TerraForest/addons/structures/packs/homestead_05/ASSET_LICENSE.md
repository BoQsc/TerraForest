Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/refrigerator.tres`, `models/kettle.tres`,
`models/cooking_pot.tres`, `models/frying_pan.tres`, `models/cutting_board.tres`,
`models/utensil_rail.tres`, `models/plate_rack.tres`,
`models/preserve_jars.tres`, `models/bread_bin.tres`, `models/grain_sack.tres`,
their embedded materials/textures, and `icons.trtex`.

These original parametric models were authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The user requested a separate Homestead 05 kitchen/pantry pack using
the proposed ten-object roster and a picture of its actual models. The brief
specifies cream enamel, faded teal, weathered timber, copper cookware and warm
food colours. No external models, images, food photographs, textures, branding
or third-party reference artwork were supplied or copied. Jar contents and
grain kernels are original generated geometry; labels contain no brand artwork.

`author.gd` retains editable geometry. It reuses the project's original 0BSD
mesh/tube helpers and CC0 procedural material patterns from Homestead 02 and
04; those terms and provenance remain intact. Runtime meshes embed their own
geometry/materials/textures. `provenance.json` records exact output and local
source hashes plus both inherited helper source hashes.

Godot's primitives and SurfaceTool are external authoring tools, not new
vendored dependencies or material covered by this dedication. Authoring,
catalog, integration and test code is original **0BSD**. License dedication
remains separate from visual review and gameplay/performance acceptance.
