Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/hall_console.tres`, `models/entry_bench.tres`,
`models/shoe_rack.tres`, `models/umbrella_stand.tres`, `models/doormat.tres`,
`models/key_rack.tres`, `models/mailbox.tres`, `models/muddy_boots.tres`,
`models/planter.tres`, `models/watering_can.tres`, their embedded materials and
textures, and `icons.trtex`.

These original parametric models were authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The user requested a separate Homestead 08 pack and a picture of
its actual models, accepting the proposed ten-object Entryway & Porch roster.
The brief specifies weathered timber, faded green metal, woven fibre and
terracotta. No external models, photographs, textures, fonts, branding or
third-party reference artwork were supplied or copied. Coir weave, foliage,
mud patches, umbrella pleats, keys and footwear are original geometry or
procedural pixels. No third-party plant or footwear design assets are included.

`author.gd` retains editable geometry. It reuses the project's original 0BSD
mesh/tube helpers and CC0 procedural materials from Homestead 02 and 04;
their terms and provenance remain intact. Runtime meshes embed their own
geometry/materials/textures. `provenance.json` records exact output and local
source hashes, including both inherited helper source hashes.

Godot's primitives, SurfaceTool and image APIs are external authoring tools,
not new vendored dependencies or material covered by this dedication. Pack
authoring, catalog, integration and test code is original **0BSD**. Asset
dedication is separate from visual review and gameplay/performance acceptance.
