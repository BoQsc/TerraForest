Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/writing_desk.tres`, `models/desk_chair.tres`,
`models/typewriter.tres`, `models/radio.tres`, `models/telephone.tres`,
`models/desk_fan.tres`, `models/documents.tres`, `models/calendar.tres`,
`models/briefcase.tres`, `models/wastebasket.tres`, their embedded
materials/textures, and `icons.trtex`.

These original parametric models were authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The user requested a separate Homestead 06 study/communications
pack using the proposed ten-object roster and a picture of its actual models.
The brief specifies dark timber, cream metal, muted teal and black plastic,
with detailed static typewriter keys, telephone dial/cord and fan guard/blades.
No external models, images, fonts, branding or third-party reference artwork
were supplied or copied. Calendar artwork uses original geometric digits and
an authored November 2026 layout. Paper markings, crumpled-paper geometry and
procedural leather grain are original work.

`author.gd` retains editable geometry and print artwork. It reuses the project's
original 0BSD mesh/tube helpers and CC0 procedural material patterns from
Homestead 02 and 04; their terms and provenance remain intact. Runtime meshes
embed their own geometry/materials/textures. `provenance.json` records exact
output and local source hashes plus both inherited helper source hashes.

Godot's primitives, SurfaceTool and image APIs are external authoring tools,
not new vendored dependencies or material covered by this dedication.
Authoring, catalog, integration and test code is original **0BSD**. License
dedication remains separate from visual review and gameplay/performance acceptance.
