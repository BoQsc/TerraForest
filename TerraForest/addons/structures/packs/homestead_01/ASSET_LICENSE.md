Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/bed.tres`, `models/nightstand.tres`,
`models/wardrobe.tres`, `models/counter.tres`, `models/cupboard.tres`,
`models/sink.tres`, `models/stove.tres`, `models/sofa.tres`, `models/books.tres`,
`models/mug.tres`, their embedded materials/textures and `icons.trtex`.

These are original parametric models authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The brief was a separate, recognizable first household pack: bed,
nightstand, wardrobe, counter, cupboard, sink, stove, sofa, books and mug, usable
as house furnishings and object-system test content. Original dimensions,
geometry and procedural material patterns are retained in `author.gd`.
No external models, images, textures, fonts or copied geometry were used.
Godot's existing mesh primitives and SurfaceTool are external authoring tools;
Godot itself and its code are not relicensed or included in this asset dedication.

`catalog.gd`, `author.gd` and the repository test/authoring driver are original
**0BSD code**. `provenance.json` records source and baked-output hashes and the
authoring command. The assets are kept together under the stable pack identity
`terraforest.homestead_01`; inventory and model identities are persistence keys.
License dedication is separate from user visual approval and performance acceptance.
