Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/workbench.tres`, `models/vise.tres`,
`models/tool_board.tres`, `models/toolbox.tres`, `models/sawhorse.tres`,
`models/plank_stack.tres`, `models/paint_tin.tres`, `models/rope.tres`,
`models/cable_spool.tres`, `models/handcart.tres`, their embedded materials
and textures, and `icons.trtex`.

These original parametric models were authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The user requested a separate Homestead 04 pack using the proposed
workshop roster, followed by a picture of its actual models. The brief specifies
weathered timber, dull iron, faded teal paint and restrained rust-red accents.
No external model, image, texture, branding or reference artwork was copied.
The hand tools on the board were authored here as decorative geometry rather
than extracted from existing held-tool art.

`author.gd` retains editable geometry, including continuous rope/cable tube
construction, vise details, open furniture frames and cart wheel spokes.
It reuses the project's original 0BSD mesh helpers and CC0 procedural patterns
from Homestead 02; those terms and provenance remain intact. The runtime meshes
have their own embedded geometry/materials/textures. `provenance.json` records
exact asset hashes and both the local author and reused helper source hashes.

Godot's primitives and SurfaceTool are external authoring tools, not new
vendored dependencies or material covered by this dedication. Authoring,
catalog, integration and test code is original **0BSD**. License dedication
remains separate from visual review and gameplay/performance acceptance.
