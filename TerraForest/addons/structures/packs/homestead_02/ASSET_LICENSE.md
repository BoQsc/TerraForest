These are original parametric models authored with Codex for TerraForest on

roster of rug, curtains/rail, framed picture, wall clock, coat rack, laundry
basket, crate, bottle, plate/bowl set and table lamp, followed by a picture of

third-party references were supplied or copied. The palette and basic mesh
helpers continue the project's original Homestead 01 contribution; its terms
and provenance remain intact. Geometry and procedural material patterns are
retained in `author.gd` and output hashes in `provenance.json`.
