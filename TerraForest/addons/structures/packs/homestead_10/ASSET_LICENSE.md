Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/dining_table.tres`,
`models/dining_chair.tres`, `models/china_cabinet.tres`,
`models/serving_trolley.tres`, `models/tea_set.tres`,
`models/serving_platter.tres`, `models/cutlery_tray.tres`,
`models/bread_basket.tres`, `models/fruit_bowl.tres`,
`models/table_linen.tres`, their embedded materials/textures, and `icons.trtex`.

These original parametric models were authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The user requested a separate Homestead 10 pack and a picture of
its actual models, accepting the proposed ten-object Dining Room & Serving
roster. The brief specifies warm oak, cream ceramics, muted green fabric and
brass. No external models, images, textile patterns, fonts, food photographs,
branding or third-party reference artwork were supplied or copied. Glass panes,
tableware profiles, hollow spout, woven basket, bread grain/scoring, fruit,
cutlery and linen details are original geometry or procedural pixels.

`author.gd` retains editable geometry. It reuses the project's original 0BSD
mesh/tube helpers and CC0 materials from Homestead 02/04, and the original
0BSD cushion helper from Homestead 07. Their terms/provenance remain intact.
Runtime meshes embed their own geometry/materials/textures. `provenance.json`
records exact output and local source hashes, including all three helper sources.

Godot's primitives and SurfaceTool are external authoring tools, not new
vendored dependencies or material covered by this dedication. Pack authoring,
catalog, integration and test code is original **0BSD**. Asset dedication is
separate from visual review and gameplay/performance acceptance.
