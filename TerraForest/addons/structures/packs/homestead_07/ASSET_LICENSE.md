Covered assets: `pack.json`, `models/armchair.tres`, `models/coffee_table.tres`,
`models/bookcase.tres`, `models/sideboard.tres`, `models/record_player.tres`,
`models/record_crate.tres`, `models/chessboard.tres`, `models/newspapers.tres`,
`models/floor_lamp.tres`, `models/fireplace.tres`, their embedded materials and
textures, and `icons.trtex`.

These original parametric models were authored with Codex for TerraForest on
2026-11-26. The user requested a separate Homestead 07 pack and a picture of
its actual models, accepting the proposed ten-object Living Room & Leisure
roster. The brief specifies warm timber, muted upholstery and brass details.
No external models, images, fonts, branding, music, newspaper articles, book
text or third-party reference artwork were supplied or copied. Book spines,
record labels, newspaper column marks and abstract artwork are original
geometry or procedural pixels. Chess pieces are independently authored
traditional geometric silhouettes, with no copied model inputs.

`author.gd` retains editable geometry and print artwork. It reuses the project's
original 0BSD mesh/tube helpers and CC0 procedural materials from Homestead 02
and 04; their terms and provenance remain intact. Runtime meshes embed their
own geometry/materials/textures. `provenance.json` records exact output and
local source hashes, including both inherited helper source hashes.

Godot's primitives, SurfaceTool and image APIs are external authoring tools,
not new vendored dependencies or material covered by this dedication. Pack
authoring, catalog, integration and test code is original **0BSD**. Asset
dedication is separate from visual review and gameplay/performance acceptance.
