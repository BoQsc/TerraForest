# Settlement actor review

Run **Review Settlement Actors.cmd** from the TerraForest folder. It opens a
separate saved copy of the furnished settlement with an actor already on the
first street. Existing gameplay saves are not edited. If the review copy does
not exist, the launcher prepares it and verifies a fresh reopen first.

Use ~ to enter development mode if needed, then choose slot9, Actors. Aim at an
actor, hold Alt and click Select/send. Release Alt to aim at nearby clear ground,
then hold Alt and click Select/send again. The ring identifies the selection;
the upper-left HUD reports planning, following, arrived or a blocked route.
Stop holds the actor at its current position. Place and Remove operate at the
aim point; Save world retains your edits in this separate review world.

Walking is currently local (16m planning extent), with a maximum16 active
collision actors near the player. The body mesh is a diagnostic mannequin.
This is not yet a complete NPC behavior or city-population system. The launcher
enables simulation on every reopen; enabling through the panel alone is a
session setting. Water is unchanged.
