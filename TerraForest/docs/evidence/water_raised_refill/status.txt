2026-10-15: Raised and partially scooped water refill regression

Human review exposed that full-unit accounting did not imply shape reversibility.
A column with an overhang loses the overhang on scoop/return and adds water above
it instead. The native regression reproduced this before the change.

Fix: bucket scoops retain per-cell missing amounts as explicit water edit state.
A nearby pour first fills these vacancies; it does not infer their shape from the
highest connected water. Any bucket can supply the water. A bounded native vacancy
ray query makes an emptied aimed surface remain targetable for pouring only.
Scoop picking still uses the actual rendered water surface.

Vacancies are lazy 512-entry uint32 arrays (2 KiB per edited 4 m region, at most
8 MiB across the existing 4096-region cap). Snapshot v9 stores only nonzero entries;
v1-v8 remain readable, and worlds without vacancies still encode byte-exact v8.
Solid-edit bounds clear affected vacancies. No automatic refill, flow, background
simulation, extra rendering, or unbounded operation history is introduced.
Refill searches at most 9^3 prepared cells. Vacancy ray query visits at most 11^3
cells for a five-metre interaction ray, only on a pour action.

Passing checks:
- water_raised_refill, debug and release: raised column/overhang at three scoop
  heights; exact per-cell restoration; v9 byte-exact reload; different full bucket;
  invalid vacancy quantity rejected atomically.
- water_raised_world: normal world, actual mouse clicks building raised water,
  three side scoop/return cycles; all cell amounts and published vertex buffers
  match their pre-scoop state. 1920x1080 fullscreen, 60 FPS cap. Captures inspected.
- water_structure_bucket: existing construction/bucket/history/paging regression.
- water_static_compound: normal compound disk save/reload regression.

Limits: existing pre-v9 holes have no recorded removed shape and still use the
older geometric refill fallback. This is bounded local targeting, not global undo
or automatic leveling. Multiple nearby holes are filled nearest first. The old
shapes can remain angular because water is static. These checks do not establish
universal interaction correctness or sustained hardware/thermal performance.
The first world cycle includes screenshot readback; its elapsed time is not a
bucket latency measurement. Recorded max native bucket action was 2.278 ms.
