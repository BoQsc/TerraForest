Sloping ground-pour integration - 2026-10-13

Runtime changes:
- Existing native water store and shared surface-nets builder now use the same
  volume-inverted sloping profile for shallow runoff, depth, meshing and bucket
  connectivity. No second water implementation or terrain mesher was introduced.
- Neighbouring equal-level lake water suppresses the reconstructed slope.
- Exact wet-face overlap respects terrain and partial vertical openings.
- Sub-millilitre transport is no longer rounded up into fast-moving droplets.
  Quantities remain in their source cell; no cleanup deletes water.
- Saturated integer-capacity cells reach the cell top despite fractional capacity
  rounding. Without this correction the visual check caught an interior sheet.
- Dirty mesh owners include the extra neighbouring-level dependency.

Verified:
- Actual-world full bucket click, 10 L natural-ground pour and visible scoop pass.
- Ground pour contains all 10,000 ml in one local connected component after 2 s.
  26 rendered top triangle centroids are above terrain by 1.3-5.5 mm.
- Inspected screenshot shows a continuous shallow patch instead of strips;
  its outline remains angular. Terrain contact sampling is not an exhaustive
  triangle/terrain intersection proof.
- The current local scoop recovers 9 L from that spread patch; 1 L remains in
  the world. Bucket neighbourhood is still bounded to two cells per axis.
- Underwater capture no longer shows the reproduced bands/shards or the
  interior sheet detected during this change.
- Native profile math, 30 material cases, 510 envelope topology patterns,
  publication/query consistency, migration, captured ground replay and flow
  timestep checks pass. Geometric connectivity checks replace the obsolete
  horizontal-head assumption; independent math tests cover their predicates.
- Full-world test passes local scoop depression/refill, shore pour-back,
  separate dug-bowl pour and full 10 L retrieval, but FAILS the existing mined
  outlet response deadline. Its failure and complete log are retained here.

Still open; this is not a completed water acceptance gate:
- Prompt flow into mined outlets and large active-front scheduling/publication.
- Arbitrary detached terrain fragments, steep/complex water contact, repeated
  edits and visible local response across all supported cases.
- Angular puddle edges; no general fragment cleanup has been implemented.
- Sub-millilitre transport is retained rather than accumulated, so very tiny
  flows can stop and depend on the timestep. No momentum solver is claimed.
- Existing 0.5 m cells, bounded flow work, gravity repair, region limits and
  lack of water paging remain. Native reconstruction adds local CPU work;
  laptop GPU/CPU/thermal sustainability has not been qualified by these tests.
- Snapshot bytes remain version 5; slopes are derived from cached terrain and
  amounts. Historical sleeping states are not universally re-equilibrated.

Build uses cached Zig and prebuilt godot-cpp; no SDK source rebuild. Original
code under project 0BSD. Test worlds are isolated and self-terminate.
