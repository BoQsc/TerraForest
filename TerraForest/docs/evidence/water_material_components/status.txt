Material-component reconstruction, 2026-10-13

Replacing old quad-count assertions with representation-independent closed-edge
and connected-component checks exposed TWO genuine failures before the fix:
diagonal wet cells became one connected surface, and a solid vertical ledge did
not separate an upper pool. Closed cross-region surfaces, conservation and
invalidation already passed the revised checks. The two failures were fixed in
the shared reconstruction machinery, not waived by changing expected outcomes.

Wet corners are partitioned through actual wet openings before reconstruction.
Each component gets its own representative. Face lookup chooses the component
containing the edge's wet endpoint, including both interfaces where wet endpoints
are separated by a solid interval. The connection policy is volume-aware;
terrain's existing density path is unchanged and uses the shared edge machinery.

Checks passed:
- All 510 non-empty 2x2x2 occupancy patterns (255 shallow, 255 full): closed,
  nondegenerate surfaces and expected connected-component counts.
- Hand-authored diagonal, cross-region and solid-ledge cases; invalid occupancy,
  boundary invalidation, exact quantities and resumable vertical repairs.
- 30 thin-water/query cases plus immutable-batch/cancellation checks.
- 72 complete terrain mesh packets unchanged versus frozen pre-refactor source.
- Actual 1920x1080 combined-world test: all 50 checks, mining, buckets, cached
  save/reload and atomic scene publication.

Not a proof for every material field or arbitrary terrain geometry. Flow remains
an approximate finite-volume relaxation; reconstructed visual volume is not an
exact geometric integral of stored millilitres. Query/display snapshot agreement,
large-scale staging cost and sustained laptop performance remain unqualified.
