Actual normal world, real bucket mouse input, dug terrain, 1920x1080 fullscreen.
The focused run stops after pouring and fails if the stored floor at any wet
cell centre differs from the terrain collision surface by more than 2 cm.
This is a centre-contact gate, not a complete shoreline/overhang qualification.

Run 20261013T113737054580Z: expected failure in 12.73 seconds, no engine errors.
10,000 ml conserved across four cells. Stored depth is 9.6--10.2 mm, while the
stored floor is 44.275 m and terrain collision at each cell centre is 44.15018 m.

The earlier full run 20261013T113550926401Z passed its existing gameplay/archive
checks, which do not enforce this terrain-contact tolerance. Passing those
checks is not evidence that the water's visual volume is correctly supported.

Cause in current code: NativeWaterTerrainPatch::sample_terrain intersects four
corner air intervals, retaining the highest corner floor throughout a cell.
WaterSurfaceInterval and build_surface then reconstruct a thin layer above that
conservative floor, without accounting for the terrain below inside the cell.
Surface-nets outline smoothing alone cannot fix that missing occupied volume.

Next correction: terrain-aware partial-cell volume/head and shared-surface
reconstruction. Preserve conservative face barriers, exact millilitre transfers,
published query/render agreement and cached loading; do not simply lower the
floor, visually inflate the water, or permit diagonal leakage. This remains
an unresolved gameplay visual/correctness issue, ahead of performance tuning.
