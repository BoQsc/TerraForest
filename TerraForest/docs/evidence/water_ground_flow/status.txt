Water ground pour and bounded lateral transfer - 2026-10-13

Implemented in the existing NativeRegionalWater runtime; shared terrain surface
reconstruction and exact integer millilitre quantities remain authoritative.

- Partial top openings now retain terrain-shaped floor capacity rather than a
  conservative completely solid cell. Ambiguous walls and ceilings still use
  conservative intervals; arbitrary terrain topology is not qualified.
- Lateral transfer is capped by elapsed time, gravity head and a shallow-flow
  bed-resistance approximation. Deep and shallow water use the same path.
  The roughness value 0.06 is a gameplay choice, not calibrated hydraulics.
  Formula reference: USACE HEC-RAS Uniform Flow Computations,
  https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/rasdocs/ras1dtechref/latest/stable-channel-design-functions/uniform-flow-computations
- Scoop rays reach terrain, rather than stopping 1 cm above it and missing
  visible shallow water. The placement clearance remains on pours.
- Save version 4 reads versions 1-3. Changed cached cut capacities wake without
  terrain resampling or changing stored quantity. Old binaries cannot read v4.

Remaining limits:
- Very shallow runoff renders as strip-like patches; visual acceptance remains
  open. A ripple texture cannot repair a geometry/contact representation issue.
- Transfer receives frame time, but active cells can wait behind the bounded work
  queue. Large active fronts can therefore slow simulation. Vertical repair is
  still prioritized and not a fully time-integrated fluid solver. One-ml rounding
  permits tiny droplets to advance faster than bulk. Not calibrated physics.
- Flow deadline stability, large connected publication latency, arbitrary cut
  topology, water paging and sustained laptop GPU/thermal behavior remain open.
- Screenshots stall frames. Their HUD FPS and these checks are not GPU-cost or
  sustained-60-FPS qualification. No additional renderer or water store added.
