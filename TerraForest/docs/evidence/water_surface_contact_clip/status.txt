2026-10-13 — Rendered surface contact clipping

Existing surface-nets triangles now undergo cell-local polygon clipping where they actually penetrate supported cached terrain floor triangles. This uses the same contact geometry as capacity/volume calculation. It neither raises vertices nor changes stored quantities. Rendered and published picking triangles come from the same resulting mesh. Planes/intersections use local doubles and fixed-size polygon buffers. A probe avoids subdividing triangles already outside terrain; unaffected triangles are emitted unchanged. No shader, new water runtime, global scan or per-frame terrain reconstruction was added.
