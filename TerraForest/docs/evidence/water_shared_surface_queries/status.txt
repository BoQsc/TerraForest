Shared surface query integration, 2026-10-13

Rendering, bucket raycasts and nearest-surface selection call build_surface.
Triangles retain their owning wet cell so bucket transfers use exact stored
quantities. Ray reach is limited to five metres; invalid terrain occupancy is
not pickable. Queries do not consume dirty render jobs or advance simulation.
The old shoreline polygon builder is removed. Physical depth uses stored wet
intervals; it no longer invents water outside them using obsolete polygons.

Release-native check: 30 height/boundary cases, nine assertions passed,
including query/mesh agreement, misses, invalid occupancy and conservation.
World check: all 49 assertions passed with actual shoreline bucket clicks,
mining, independent puddle, conserved quantities, save and cached reload.
The shore test now requires a reconstructed surface hit and a nearby outward
miss, rather than assuming the obsolete clipped apron outside wet cells exists.

Not completed: coherent mesh publication during flow, existing continuity
failures, material storage unification and broad performance qualification.
Queries use current state, not a cached copy of the last published mesh.
They rebuild nearby bounded regions on demand; no per-frame raycast was added.
