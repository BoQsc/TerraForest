Terrain/water contact artifacts - 2026-10-13

The user's underwater screenshot was reproduced in the normal temporary bucket
review world. The baseline shows horizontal water-colored bands and reflective
fragments on the bed. The corresponding after view no longer shows those bands
or fragments. These reproduced pieces were rendered water/solid contact faces;
this does not establish that all detached terrain components are corrected.

Changes:
- NativeRegionalWater's existing shared surface builder distinguishes exposed
  water/air interfaces from water/solid contacts using the cached cell profile.
  Contact faces are omitted from runtime mesh uploads and bucket query geometry.
  No shader discard, deletion of stored water, extra renderer or alternate mesh
  implementation is used. Free falling water's air-facing surfaces remain.
- Boundary representatives at a partial solid floor meet that floor instead of
  averaging side crossings halfway above it.
- Bucket pours now preserve immutable mesh-region data BEFORE changing amount.
  Previously only removals preserved the in-flight snapshot at this point.
- next_mesh(true) is a diagnostic flag on the same builder: it includes contact
  faces to retain closed-envelope topology audits. Runtime publication and
  raycasts use exposed faces. Closed-volume assertions are not claims that an
  exposed water surface must also draw its buried bed.

Validation:
- 30 thin-volume placements and existing published-query consistency tests pass.
- New tests: solid-enclosed water renders only the air-facing top; buried contact
  surfaces are not bucket targets; geometry never changes stored quantity; an
  actual 10 L bucket pour preserves all packets of an already-started batch.
- All 510 shallow/full stored-envelope patterns retain manifold edges and the
  expected number of components. Cut-cell migration and volume tests pass.
- Short 1920x1080 normal-world visual capture and bucket contact check pass.
  Natural-ground pour/scoop transfers also pass but their visual result does NOT.

Blocking work remains:
- Natural-ground shallow runoff remains strip-like (runoff_still_open.png).
- Arbitrary shore gaps/contact, disconnected small material fragments and clear
  sustained local scoop/refill feedback are not qualified or marked complete.
- No terrain-fragment cleanup was implemented in this change. A future local
  cleanup must preserve legitimate placed/separate volumes and material quantity.
- Prior flow-deadline instability remains open. No thermal/performance claim is
  inferred from screenshot FPS. No broad long-duration benchmark was run.
