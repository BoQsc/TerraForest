# Underground geology checkpoint

Run `Play Geology Checkpoint.cmd` from TerraForest. It opens a separate locally saved world in gameplay mode, at the iron tunnel entrance, facing into the tunnel. Your settlement checkpoints are unchanged. The launcher requires the local save; it does not regenerate or overwrite a missing checkpoint.

Press **F** to turn on the headlamp, then walk forward along the sloping tunnel. The grey iron vein is at the end. Mine with the terrain tool; use **Tab > Pending materials** to claim resources and craft metal. **F5** saves. The headlamp starts off each launch and uses one 14 m shadowed engine spotlight; its GPU cost has not yet been qualified.

A second authored tunnel exposes copper. Its entrance is at [328.0, 90.28734, 332.0], with ore at [368.0, 109.0, 332.0]. Both entrance-to-face routes passed automated on-foot movement without jumping, including after a fresh-process reload. The terrain between the two entrances has not been route-tested.

These are authored access tunnels to seeded deposits, not proof of natural cave discovery. Ore colour and texture are visible, but exposed faces remain visibly faceted. Mining continuity and sustained frame/thermal qualification remain open. The screenshots' 60 FPS HUD is not a performance test.
